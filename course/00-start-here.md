# The Community Loyalty Engine

> **Free Course — Transform Your Community from Passive to Active**

---

## Welcome!

You're about to learn the psychology and mechanics behind **high-retention communities**.

Most online communities fail for the same reason: they're built for *passive consumption*. Members join, scroll, and eventually drift away. No connection. No investment. No reason to stay.

This course teaches you how to flip that dynamic—building communities where members actively contribute, compete, and connect.

By the end, you'll have:
- A working XP and leveling system
- Leaderboards that drive healthy competition
- Badges that celebrate real achievements
- A foundation for advanced engagement strategies
